# The ITP System — Three Phases Explained
The Inspection & Test Plan (ITP) is the heart of the QMP Inspect. It is not just a document — it is a live project quality system that runs through three distinct phases from start to finish. Understanding these three phases is essential before you start building.
[TABLE]
Phase | Name | What happens | Output
1 | Development | Build the ITP — add sections, activities, acceptance criteria, point types, evidence types. Go through approval workflow. Get all five sign-offs. Export the blank ITP PDF for client review. | Approved ITP document signed by all parties
2 | Execution | Use the ITP on site. Each activity opens the correct evidence form — checklist, document upload, or measurement record. Inspector signs digitally. Hold points release work. NCRs raised on failures. Progress tracked live. | Complete inspection records for every activity
3 | Handover | All activities complete. Compile the full records dossier. Issue Handover Certificate. Client formally receives the quality records package. ITP closed. | Records dossier + Handover Certificate
[/TABLE]
## ITP Structure — Sections and Activities
Every ITP is structured with section groupings and numbered activities underneath. This matches how real ITPs work on Oil & Gas, concrete, and construction projects globally.
[TABLE]
Section | Default name | Typical activities
1 | Submittals | Material submittals, mix design approval, drawing approval, method statement approval, ITP approval
2 | Materials | Material receiving inspection — certs, markings, physical condition — per delivery
3 | Execution | All in-process inspection activities — reinforcement, formwork, pre-pour, welding, dimensional checks, coatings
4 | Testing & Reports | Concrete cube results, pressure test records, NDE reports, third-party test reports
5 | Documentation & Closeout | As-built surveys, records dossier compilation, final sign-off
[/TABLE]
Sections are fully customisable — rename, add, delete, and reorder. Designed for any discipline: concrete, steel, electrical, mechanical, piping, coating.
## The Three Evidence Types
[TABLE]
Type | Name | Inspector sees on site | Examples
1 | Checklist | Structured list of items — each tapped Pass/Fail/N/A. Comments required on any Fail. Mandatory items must all be answered. Signed digitally. Any Fail = activity Fail. | Pre-pour concrete inspection, reinforcement receiving, formwork check, weld visual
2 | Document upload | Upload PDF or photo. Enter document reference and date. Confirm meets requirement — Yes/No/Conditional. If No or Conditional — comments mandatory. | Concrete test report, material test certificate, drawing approval, calibration certificate
3 | Measurement record | Table of measurements — inspector enters actuals. System auto-calculates Pass/Fail vs nominal ± tolerance. Any row Fail = overall Fail. | Reinforcement cover depth, slab thickness, slump test, concrete temperature, DFT thickness
[/TABLE]
## Point Types — What Each One Means
[TABLE]
Code | Name | Rule | System behaviour
H | Hold point | Work CANNOT proceed until Jags Assurance inspector physically witnesses and signs off | Red badge, red row background. Blocks progress. IRN offered on pass. Email alert to inspector.
W | Witness point | Inspector should attend but can issue written release if unavailable | Orange badge, amber row background. Inspector prompted to attend or issue release.
R | Review point | Inspector reviews documents remotely — no physical presence needed | Blue badge, white row. Inspector reviews uploaded document and signs remotely.
S | Surveillance | Periodic monitoring — not tied to one specific activity | Green badge. Linked to surveillance schedule.
RE | Record only | For information and record-keeping — no inspector action needed | Grey badge. Auto-completes when evidence uploaded. No signature required.
[/TABLE]

# Section 8 — How Reports Work

[TABLE]

1 | You click Generate Report | You press the button
2 | App collects data from database | Gathers everything needed: project, ITP items, results, photos, signatures
3 | Data is sent to Puppeteer | Puppeteer is the PDF engine built into the app

5 | Template is converted to PDF | The filled template is printed as a PDF with your branding
6 | PDF sent to your browser | The PDF appears — you click Download
