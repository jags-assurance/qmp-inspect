PROJECT SPECIFICATION — LV SWITCHROOM REFURBISHMENT
Document No: MER-SPEC-ELEC-004   Rev: 2   Date: 2026-09-01
Client: Meridian Energy   Discipline: Electrical

1. SCOPE
This specification covers the supply, installation, testing and commissioning of a new
415V low-voltage (LV) switchboard and associated outgoing feeders in the main switchroom,
including cable termination, insulation resistance testing, protective device settings,
and final functional testing prior to energization.

2. APPLICABLE STANDARDS
- IEC 61439-1/2 — Low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies
- IEC 60364 — Electrical installations of buildings
- Client Electrical Installation Standard MER-ES-100 Rev 3

3. MATERIALS AND SUBMITTALS
3.1 The switchgear nameplate rating shall be verified against the approved submittal
    (drawing MER-DWG-E-201) before installation proceeds. Any deviation shall be raised
    as a non-conformance before work continues.
3.2 All cable and cable gland materials shall match the approved material submittal
    schedule MER-MAT-004.

4. INSTALLATION
4.1 Cable terminations at the switchgear shall be torqued to the manufacturer's
    specified values and visually inspected for correct lug crimping, gland sealing,
    and cable segregation (power vs control).
4.2 Earthing and bonding connections shall be installed and visually verified for
    continuity path integrity before energization.

5. TESTING AND COMMISSIONING
5.1 Insulation resistance (IR) testing shall be performed on each of the three (3)
    outgoing feeders (F1, F2, F3) at 500V DC, minimum acceptable value 100 megohms,
    per IEC 60364-6. Results shall be recorded on the IR test record.
5.2 Protective device (MCCB/relay) settings shall be verified against the approved
    protection coordination study MER-PCS-002 before the panel is energized.
5.3 A final functional test of the switchgear panel — confirming correct operation of
    all breakers, indication lamps, and interlocks — shall be carried out and witnessed
    prior to handover.
5.4 Phase rotation and voltage balance across all three phases shall be verified and
    recorded at first energization.

6. HOLD POINTS
- Cable termination and IR testing (Section 5.1) is a MANDATORY HOLD POINT for the
  Main Contractor and JAGS Assurance — work shall not proceed to energization without
  written sign-off.
- Final functional test (Section 5.3) is a WITNESS POINT for the Client.

7. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA SUMMARY
- Nameplate rating matches approved submittal — no deviation.
- IR test result >= 100 megohms per feeder, all three feeders.
- Protection settings match the approved coordination study, verified before energization.
- Functional test: all breakers operate correctly, no fault indications, interlocks function.
- Phase rotation correct (R-Y-B), voltage balance within +/-2% across phases.
